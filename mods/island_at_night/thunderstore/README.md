# Island at Night

A mod for [Snap64 Recomp](https://github.com/JackandBeans/Snap64Recomp), the
native PC port of Pokémon Snap.

The Beach, the River, the Volcano and the Valley after dark, or at sunset.
Each course's sky, ground, water and light are the course's own, turned to
night as the course loads: stars that twinkle between the clouds, a moon over
the open sky with its glint on the water, now and then a shooting star, and
the Volcano lit by its own lava. The Beach, the River and the Valley can have
their night clear, rainy, or stormy with lightning and thunder, and the Valley
can have snow that falls and lies on the land. Photos taken at night are
night wherever the game shows them, with the weather of their instant.

## Options

In the game, open Options > Mods, select Island at Night and press Z.

- **Beach, River, Volcano, Valley**: Night (the default), Sunset, or Day as
  the cartridge has it.
- **Beach Weather, River Weather**: Clear, Rain, or Storm. The River's
  default is Rain.
- **Valley Weather**: Clear, Rain, Storm, or Snow.
- **Effects at Night**: splashes, dust and mist take the night's light (the
  default) or keep their day colors.
- **Water Glow**: the moon's glint on the water and the River's fireflies.
- **Moonlight on Pokémon**: the scene's light leans toward the moon, or
  stays where the game puts it.

## Installing

Mod managers do not support Snap64 Recomp. Use **Manual Download** on this
page.

Snap64 Recomp 1.1.0 or later: drop the downloaded zip on the game window, or
put the zip in the port's `mods` folder (beside `Snap64Recomp.exe`; on a Mac,
in `~/Library/Application Support/Snap64 Recomp/`). The mod loads the next
time the game starts. Options > Mods turns it on or off.
