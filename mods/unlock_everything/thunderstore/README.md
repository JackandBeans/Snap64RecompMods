# Unlock Everything

A mod for [Snap64 Recomp](https://github.com/JackandBeans/Snap64Recomp), the
native PC port of Pokémon Snap.

Every course is open from the start. The Apple, the Pester Ball, the Poké
Flute and the Dash Engine are in hand, and the Beach tutorial is marked
done. Your Pokémon Report and your album are not touched.

The mod sets these in the save the game keeps in memory, through the game's
own functions, so they stay once you save at the lab. That cannot be undone
by turning the mod off. Copy the port's `saves` folder (beside `Snap64Recomp.exe`; on a Mac, in
`~/Library/Application Support/Snap64 Recomp/`)
somewhere safe first if you may want to go back.

## Installing

Mod managers do not support Snap64 Recomp. Use **Manual Download** on this
page.

- Snap64 Recomp 1.1.0 or later: drop the downloaded zip on the game window,
  or put the zip in the port's `mods` folder (beside `Snap64Recomp.exe`;
  on a Mac, in `~/Library/Application Support/Snap64 Recomp/`). The mod
  loads the next time the game starts.
- Snap64 Recomp 1.0.9: open the zip and put
  `snap64_unlock_everything.nrm` in the `mods` folder.

Options > Mods turns the mod on or off.
