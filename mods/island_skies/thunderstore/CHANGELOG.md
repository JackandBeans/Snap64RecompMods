# Changelog

## 0.5.0

- The first release, as Island Skies: the Beach, the River, the Volcano and
  the Valley at night, at sunset, at dawn, through a dusk, or at the hour
  your clock says, with weather and its wind, the moon's glint on the water,
  the River's fireflies, the Volcano's embers and ash, insects in the dark
  and a shooting star now and then. Simple set-up is three rows; Custom
  gives each course its own hour and weather.
