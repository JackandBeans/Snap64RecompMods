# Changelog

## 0.5.0

- The first release, as Island Skies: the Beach, the Tunnel, the River, the
  Volcano and the Valley at night, at sunset, at dawn, through a dusk, or
  live by your own clock round the whole day with the weather drawn for
  each ride; rain, storms, snow and ash with their wind, the moon's glint
  on the water, the River's fireflies, the Volcano's embers and ash,
  insects in the dark and a shooting star now and then; the moon's phase
  by the date, and the sun's hours by a latitude. Simple set-up is
  three rows, Live is one; Custom gives each course its own hour and
  weather.
